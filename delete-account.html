Delete Your Account — Battle Buddy Tactical, LLC

This page explains how to request deletion of your Battle Buddy Tactical Fire account and associated data, developed and published by Battle Buddy Tactical, LLC.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to battlebuddytfapp@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Battle Buddy Tactical Fire account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete My Account"
  3. Include the email address registered on your account in the body of the message.

What gets deleted

Upon a verified deletion request, we permanently delete:

  • Your account profile, including your name, email address, and profile picture
  • Incidents and pre-plans you personally created and saved that are not shared with a department

What may be retained

If you are a member of a fire department using Battle Buddy Tactical Fire, pre-plans and CAD notes you contributed to that department's shared records may remain accessible to other members of that department after your account is deleted, since this information is treated as shared department infrastructure data rather than personal data. Your personal profile information (name, email) is removed from these records upon deletion.

Processing time

Deletion requests are processed within 5 business days of a verified request.
