Delete Your Account

Battle Buddy Tactical Fire

You can delete your Battle Buddy Tactical Fire account and personal data at any time, either in the app or by emailing us.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Battle Buddy Tactical Fire and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Enter your password, type DELETE, and confirm.

If you have an active subscription, cancel it first under Manage Subscription in the same Settings tab.

Option 2: By email

If you no longer have the app, email battlebuddytfapp@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request" from the email address on your account. We'll confirm the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in
  • Your profile (name, department, FDID)
  • Private pre-plans and their photos
  • Saved incidents, calls, and CAD notes
  • Your department memberships

What we keep

  • Pre-plans you shared with your department or made public. These are part of the department's records and stay available to its crews. They may still show the name of the person who created them.
  • Payment and billing records that we're required to keep for tax and accounting purposes. Card details are held by our payment processor, Stripe, not by us.
