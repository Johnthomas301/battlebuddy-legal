Privacy Policy

Battle Buddy Tactical Fire  ·  Effective August 21, 2026  ·  Last updated August 21, 2026

Battle Buddy Tactical Fire (“the App”) is developed and operated by John McGowan (“we,” “us,” “the Developer”). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Contact for any privacy question or request: battlebuddytfapp@gmail.com

Beta Software Notice

Battle Buddy Tactical Fire is currently distributed as beta software for evaluation and feedback purposes.

The App is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind. Building data, occupancy classifications, construction types, hydrant locations, and hydrant characteristics are estimates derived from third-party and public data sources. They may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect.

The App is not a substitute for on-scene size-up, department pre-planning procedures, or professional judgment. Do not rely on it as the sole basis for any operational or tactical decision. Verify all information independently.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Name
  • Fire department name
  • FDID number
  • Password (stored and managed by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your plaintext password)

When you use the App, we store content you create:

  • Pre-plans, including addresses, building dimensions, occupancy and construction classifications, hazard notes, Knox Box locations, utility shutoff notes, occupant notes, and any other text you enter
  • Incident records generated when you view an incident summary
  • CAD notes you create
  • Hydrant records and status tags you add or edit
  • Department information, if you create or join a department (department name, region, invite code, membership and role)
  • Profile photo, if you upload one

Information collected automatically

  • Location: With your permission, the App reads your device’s last known location to center the map on your area. Location is used on-device to position the map and to find nearby hydrants. We do not store a history of your movements.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: The App uses Firebase Crashlytics. If the App crashes, Crashlytics collects a stack trace, device model, operating system version, and app state at the time of the crash so we can fix the problem.
  • Coordinates attached to your content: When you save a pre-plan or incident, the building’s coordinates and the nearest hydrant’s coordinates are saved as part of that record.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect contacts, photos (other than a profile photo you choose to upload), microphone audio, or SMS
  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not use your data for advertising
  • We do not run third-party advertising networks in the App

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account and sign you in
  • To store and display the pre-plans, notes, incidents, and hydrant data you create
  • To share department-visibility content with other verified members of your department
  • To center the map near you and to identify the nearest hydrant
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • To contact you about the beta program, if you are a beta participant

Data Sharing and Visibility

Content you create has one of three visibility settings:

  • Private: visible only to you
  • Department: visible to verified members of your department
  • Public: visible to any signed-in user of the App, intended for mutual-aid sharing

Choose the visibility level appropriate for the sensitivity of the information. Do not enter information about building occupants that you are not authorized to record or share.

We do not sell or rent your information. We disclose information only:

  • To the third-party service providers listed above, to operate the App
  • If required by law, valid legal process, or a lawful government request
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of users or the public

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and content while your account is active.

To delete your account and associated data, email battlebuddytfapp@gmail.com with the subject line “Delete My Data.” We will delete your account, pre-plans, incidents, notes, and profile within 30 days of receiving your request and confirm by email.

Note: content you shared with a department or set to public may remain visible to that department if it has become part of their shared records. Tell us in your request if you want that content removed as well.

Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics per Google’s retention schedule (currently 90 days).

Security

  • Data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are hashed and managed by Google Firebase Authentication; we never have access to them
  • Database access is governed by server-side security rules that restrict reads and writes to authorized users
  • Your data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use the App at your own risk.

Your Choices

  • Location: You can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings. The App will fall back to a default map view and remain usable.
  • Profile photo: Optional.
  • Content: You can delete individual pre-plans, incidents, and notes from within the App.
  • Account: You can request full deletion at any time (see Data Retention above).

Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for use by fire service personnel and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect any revision. Material changes will be communicated to beta participants by email.
